• Paste a path: paste a path into Recent Locations (Ctrl+Shift+G) to go to a folder or open a file.
  • Settings Backup: export your settings from the About page and import them on another Mac; SameKeys backs up the current settings before an import.
  • Hide the menu bar icon: turn off Show in Menu Bar on the Overview page and SameKeys keeps working; open it again from Applications or Spotlight to get its window back.
  • A new icon: the app and the menu bar now show the new ∞ icon.
