The first release of KeyBridge: your Windows shortcuts and mouse habits, on your Mac.

  • Windows shortcuts: Ctrl+C, V, Z and the rest, Home and End, Ctrl+arrows by word, Alt+Tab, Alt+F4, Ctrl+Shift+Esc and PrtScn. They come in groups you can switch on and off, change, or add to.
  • Ctrl or fn: press Ctrl as on Windows, or use fn instead and keep Ctrl for the Mac. In Terminal, Ctrl+C still stops the running program.
  • Mouse: side buttons go back and forward, Ctrl+scroll zooms the page, and the wheel scrolls the Windows way while the trackpad stays as it is.
  • Finder: Delete, F2 and Enter work as on Windows, Ctrl+X and Ctrl+V move files, and the right-click menu can create a new document, copy a path or open Terminal.
  • Windows: ⌥+arrows snaps the front window to half the screen or fills it, as Win+arrows does on Windows.
  • Clipboard history: ⇧⌘C shows what you copied recently, like Win+V.
  • Open and save dialogs: Ctrl+G jumps to the folder open in Finder, and Ctrl+Shift+G lists recent and favorite folders.
  • English, 简体中文 and 日本語. From now on KeyBridge keeps itself up to date.
