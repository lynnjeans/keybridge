• Win+V opens the clipboard history: with Windows key shortcuts on, every keyboard opens it with its own Win key and V. Mouse buttons and custom rules can open it too.
  • Record Shortcuts: when a shortcut does not do what you expect, start recording on the About page, press it again, then export the diagnostics to see which keyboard it came from and what it became. Only shortcuts are noted, never what you type.
  • A shorter menu bar menu: the master switch is now called Enable KeyBridge and shows a status only when paused or when something is wrong. Pause for 5 minutes, 15 minutes or an hour; to stop KeyBridge for longer, switch it off.
